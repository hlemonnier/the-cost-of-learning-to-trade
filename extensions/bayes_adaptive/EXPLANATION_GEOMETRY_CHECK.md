# Physical-mesh error-envelope correction

Recorded 27 September 2026 before changing `explain.error_envelopes`. The v3
solver and `GEOMETRY_AMENDMENT.md` are already frozen for a running numerical
refinement and are not changed by this correction. No policy, numerical gate,
candidate states, ranking, seed or statistical decision is changed.

## Failure modes declared before the correction

1. Reusing `1/(W-1)+1/(B-1)` on endpoint-resolved axes understates their largest
   physical cells, which are near conditional probability one half. Resolving
   the endpoints can improve empirical control accuracy while making the broad
   global interpolation allowance larger. These are different quantities.
2. Substituting the minimum, mean or sine-parameter cell width is invalid in
   the global recurrence. Equations (28) and (31) require the maximum over
   physical grid cells of `Delta_w + max(Delta_b0,Delta_b1)`.
3. Using an unauthenticated geometry name, misspelling a supported name, or
   silently falling back to a uniform mesh can understate the allowance.
4. Changing the old uniform formula or metadata can silently relabel retained
   evidence. Original default and explicitly uniform records must remain equal.
5. Omitting the final off-grid interpolation term, using the wrong horizon or
   payoff span, or emitting a nonfinite/negative allowance invalidates the
   conservative calculation. Horizon zero must have zero allowance.
6. Better width accounting does not certify floating quadrature, special
   functions, optimization or arithmetic. Every existing `certified_bound=false`
   and excluded-error qualification must remain unchanged.
7. Editing the candidate generator or restamping historical manifests would
   contaminate fixed search provenance. The correction changes only envelope
   evaluation; generated candidate arrays and selection rules remain unchanged.

## Implementation and prewritten bounded E2E

For `belief_geometry=endpoint_sine`, read the physical axes from the solver's
immutable axis generator, record their existing geometry identity, and calculate
the maximum of adjacent-node differences separately for all three axes. The
product grid's global width is the maximum weight width plus the greater of the
two maximum conditional widths. Unknown geometries fail explicitly. For an
omitted geometry or explicit `uniform`, preserve the exact existing arithmetic
and output fields.

`verify_explanation_geometry.py --prepare` records the current uniform outputs
before implementation. After the correction, its `--verify` stage invokes the
real envelope code in fresh processes, requires those uniform records to remain
identical, independently reconstructs endpoint widths from trigonometric axes,
independently integrates Gaussian transport distance, and recalculates every
envelope at horizons 0 through 30. It verifies finite nonnegative allowances,
the zero terminal case, unchanged uncertainty flags, and rejection of an unknown
geometry. Full raw JSON outputs and a hash-bound receipt are retained. No control
family or economic experiment is generated by this E2E.

The explanation source fingerprint changes, so future candidate manifests must
be newly generated under the current source; this does not authorize modifying
historical candidates or their bytes. The original candidate design and actual
NPZ hashes must continue to agree across the reconstruction.
