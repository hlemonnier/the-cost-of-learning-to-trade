# Pilot portability verification contract (PER-503)

Scope: compare the original 90 public pilot archives in `outputs/full/pilots/` with deterministic regeneration from the committed source, protocol, and manifest seeds. The detailed diagnosis concerns `03_01` and `08_02`. A regenerated archive is a new computation, not the independent reviewer's unavailable scratch array. This exercise cannot retrospectively compare those scratch arrays.

Failure modes to check before interpreting numerical differences:

1. Wrong source, protocol, environment order, or seed produces a plausible but unrelated pilot. Require source hash and manifest protocol identity, fixed seed derivation, 100 episodes and 300 periods, and correct nine-environment order.
2. ZIP bytes can differ through metadata or compression while array contents agree. Record archive SHA-256 separately from a canonical hash of public field names, shapes, dtypes, and contiguous bytes. Do not infer field equality from archive hashes.
3. A missing, extra, mislabeled, reshaped, or retyped field can masquerade as a small numerical delta. Require the exact public field set, shape and dtype per field before elementwise comparison.
4. RNG drift can change exogenous shocks or uniform action draws, which can cascade through fills and inventory. Require exact discrete `signal`, `inventory`, `actions`, `submitted`, `fills`, and `executed_quantities` across builds. Any difference fails regardless of float closeness.
5. A float change near a fill threshold can change a boolean fill without changing a random stream. Exact discrete equality remains required; report the smallest available threshold margin where possible, but do not infer latent stream equality from public data.
6. Cash and price recur over 300 periods and may accumulate roundoff; execution prices and fees depend on the selected action. Require every public floating entry finite where applicable, with NaNs only at unexecuted `execution_prices` positions and identical NaN masks. Compare finite values with predeclared absolute limits: `1e-9` for `price`, `cash`, and `execution_prices`; `1e-12` for `return_` and `fees`; `1e-14` for `action_probability`. These are numerical-portability limits, not statistical confidence intervals. A 300-step sequence of float64 operations around a 1000-unit price has a pessimistic rounding scale around 300 × 1000 × machine epsilon = 6.7e-11; the 1e-9 bound allows additional operation-order effects but is far below a cent. The direct-return and fee limits are tighter because they do not accumulate large price levels. Also report maximum absolute and ULP differences and counts.
7. A posterior may amplify tiny public float differences or SciPy special-function rounding. Fit the same finite grid on each public pilot and require identical candidate order, same winning model, and maximum absolute `log_weights` difference no greater than `1e-9`. This permits the independently reported 7.28e-12 scale with margin but rejects a meaningful likelihood shift. Report all nine deltas, never only the winner. Stored original fit and regenerated fit must be compared under the same criterion.
8. Comparing a current archive against itself would pass vacuously. Require the regeneration runtime to be recorded and archive/field hashes to be derived from bytes read separately; include 90-file counts and two-build comparison if a distinct installed stack is accessible.
9. A macOS two-build pass cannot establish Linux x86 byte identity or recreate reviewer scratch arrays. Record platform and dependency versions explicitly and classify any Linux-specific mechanism as a hypothesis until its arrays are available.

The verifier will fail closed on schema, discrete, nonfinite, tolerance, or posterior violations. It will emit a JSON receipt with input SHA-256, runtime versions, per-field fingerprints and difference summaries, and a compact text fingerprint export suitable for a GitHub-only reviewer. Canonical `outputs/full/` files are read-only for this check.
